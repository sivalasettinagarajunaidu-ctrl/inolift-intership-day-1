About This System

Data Source

This system utilizes data derived from the CDC's Behavioral Risk Factor Surveillance System (BRFSS) 2015 survey. The original dataset contains 441,455 individuals with 330 features, cleaned down to 21 essential health indicator features for diabetes prediction.

Health Indicators Tracked

  • Blood Pressure Status
  • Cholesterol Levels
  • BMI Category
  • Smoking Status
  • Physical Activity
  • Alcohol Consumption
  • General Health
  • Mental Health Days
  • Physical Health Days
  • And more...
